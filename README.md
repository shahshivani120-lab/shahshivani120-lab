## 📂 Case studies

### SRA HQ — Organic search + AI visibility
- **Problem:** A respected firm with almost no search visibility; it wasn't in the answers law-firm leaders found on Google or in AI assistants.
- **What I did:** Rebuilt content and technical SEO, mapped buyer questions into comparison, buyer-guide and research content, and tracked AI citations in Bing Webmaster Tools.
- **Result:** Clicks up 166% while impressions rose 19%; CTR 0.4% → 0.9%; avg. position 8.5 → 6.8. SRA became the source Copilot quoted for buyer questions in its category. ChatGPT referrals engaged at 34% vs 19% from Google.

### RestoGenius — Marketing for 7 restaurant clients
- **Process:** Research → Audit → Plan → Create → Advertise → Report.
- **Websites & SEO:** Three new restaurant sites ranked within 3 months (avg. position 5.9 for the two largest).
- **Meta Ads:** Costs ranged ~10x across 19 campaigns; testing hooks and offers before scaling brought the best campaigns to <40% of the account average.
- **Funnel (GA4):** Organic visitors enquired at 2.12% vs 0.55% for direct, but only ~8% reached pricing, so I prioritised CTA hierarchy, navigation, UTMs and qualified-lead tracking.
